Nepenthes rajah -- Borneo

The King of Pitcher Plants...

🔍Quick Facts

  • Scientific Name:

    Nepenthes rajah

  • Family:

    Nepenthaceae

  • Native Region:

    Slopes of Mount Kinabalu and Mount Tambuyukon, Borneo

  • Plant Type:

    Carnivorous pitcher plant

🧪Unique Features

  • Massive pitchers can hold up to 3.5 liters of fluid.
  • Known to trap:
    • Insects
    • Spiders
    • Small lizards
    • Small mammals

🧠Fun Fact

Nepenthes rajah is protected under international law (CITES Appendix I) due to its rarity and vulnerability in the wild.
