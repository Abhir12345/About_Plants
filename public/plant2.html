Amorphophallus titanum (Titan Arum) -- Indonesia

Also known as the Titan Arum, this plant boasts the largest unbranched inflorescence in the world, reaching heights of over 3 meters (10 feet). Native to Sumatra, it emits a potent odor resembling rotting meat to attract carrion insects for pollination. The bloom is rare and short-lived, making it a significant event when it occurs. ​
